Apple is arguably the most environmentally conscious and transparent of the major phone-makers, ranking hightest in sustainability, globally. However they rely on an agressive product launch and upgrade model, aside from open source concerns.

The Google Pixels (models 6 through 10, the Fold and the Tablet) are commonly reused for GrapheneOS, the free, open source, de-googled, privacy-centric Android OS.

For anyone prioritizing eco-friendliness over de-Googleization, check out the Nothing Phone (2a), The Apple iPhone 15 Pro, the Nokia G22 and Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra. These rank high in eco-friendliness for their repairability, recycled and reused materials, as well as product availability and support (varies per model). The BEST environmental option is always to use or fix the phone you already have.
